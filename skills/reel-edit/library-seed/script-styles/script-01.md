name: Moment → cost → turn → action
kind: script-style
source: built-in
---
1. **Moment** the viewer recognises: "I have [too many X] and still can't [Y]."
2. **Cost** with a real number: "Last week that cost me [number]."
3. **Turn**: "I thought the problem was [what everyone blames]. It was [what I found]."
4. **Action** for tonight: "Take 5 minutes and [one clear action]."
Carry ONE running example through every step; show progress on screen (step dots, a number that climbs); put the
viewer's objection on screen ("so what?") and answer it; end with a saveable recap and one ask.

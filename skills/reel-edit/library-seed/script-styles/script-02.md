id: script-02
name: Double Tap explainer
kind: script-style
source: built-in
---
Hook in 2 s (≤ 8 words with a name or number), one idea per reel, 110–140 words (25–45 s).
Say every technical idea twice: precise first, then in 3rd-grade words or a metaphor.
Re-hook at 30–40% ("But here's the catch…"). Numbers beat adjectives; every claim has proof on screen.
The last line echoes a word from the hook. One ask (comment word or "send this to …").
